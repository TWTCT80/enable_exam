How this runs

  • Answers are final. Once you confirm, the item is scored and you move on. There is no back, no skip, no flagging.
  • The clock does not pause. When it reaches zero the attempt is submitted as it stands.
  • You will not see your score until the attempt ends.
  • Mark an item unsure if you guessed or hesitated. It changes nothing about scoring and does not let you return to the item — it only means the review afterwards explains that item too, instead of assuming a correct answer needs no explanation.
  • Keyboard: 1–5 or A–E to select, U to mark unsure, Enter to confirm.
